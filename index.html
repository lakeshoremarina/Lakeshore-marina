About Grand Lake

Ohio's Largest Inland Lake.

Grand Lake St. Marys is Ohio's largest inland lake at 13,500 acres with 52 miles of shoreline — and it's right in your backyard. With no horsepower limits and wide open water, it's one of the best boating lakes in the Midwest.

Originally built entirely by hand between 1837 and 1845 as a feeder reservoir for the Miami & Erie Canal, Grand Lake holds the distinction of being the world's largest lake ever dug by hand. Today it's a beloved recreation destination for fishing, boating, tubing, wakeboarding, and more.
